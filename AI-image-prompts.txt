ORGANIKANE — AI IMAGE PROMPT SHEET
=================================

Use this with any image generator (Midjourney, DALL-E / ChatGPT, Ideogram, Firefly, Flux, Gemini...).
The goal is a set of consistent, clean product photos that drop straight into the website.

1. STYLE GUIDE (paste at the end of EVERY prompt)
-------------------------------------------------
"Soft natural daylight, subtle contact shadow, seamless warm off-white background (#F3F6EE), shot slightly above eye level, shallow depth of field, clean editorial product photography, eco-friendly premium feel, no text, no logos, no people, no watermark, 4:5 ratio, high detail"

For a transparent look, ask for "plain white background" and then remove the background (remove.bg, Photoshop, Canva, or Adobe Express - free).

2. HOW TO ADD A PHOTO TO THE SITE
---------------------------------
a) Remove the background and export as WebP or PNG, about 1000 px wide.
b) Save it in:  images/products/   (for example images/products/paper-straw-8mm-black.webp)
c) Open js/products-data.js, find that product and add one line:
       photo: 'images/products/paper-straw-8mm-black.webp',
   Done. The site now uses your photo instead of the illustration. Nothing else to change.
   (Two products already use photos: bagasse straws and bagasse plates & containers.)

Tips: keep every product the same size on the canvas, same camera angle and the same light. That consistency is what makes the catalogue look expensive.

6. WHAT TO CHECK BEFORE PUBLISHING
----------------------------------
- Straw counts and cup sizes in the picture should match the product (8 mm is thicker than 6 mm; 8 oz is smaller than 12 oz).
- Compostable or recyclable claims should match what your supplier certifies.
- AI images are for illustration. Real photos of your own stock are always the best final step.
